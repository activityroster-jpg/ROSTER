## Channel description

ActivityRoster is staff rostering for RYA sailing and watersports centres.

Your courses, instructors, qualifications and ratios in one place. Auto-roster fills your week with people who are free and qualified for each session, and the checks catch clashes, double bookings, lapsed licences and short ratios before they become a problem on the water. Your instructors use the app to set their availability, keep their licences up to date, ask for leave and see their shifts.

This channel has short how-to videos for every part of ActivityRoster, in the order a centre sets up: from signing up to building, confirming and printing your roster, leave, pay and the instructor app.

Built for sailing schools, watersports centres, clubs and activity centres in the UK and Ireland. Your first month is free: https://activityroster.com
Step-by-step guides: https://activityroster.com/learn

## ActivityRoster in two minutes

Running a sailing or watersports centre means juggling courses, instructors, qualifications and ratios, usually on a spreadsheet and a busy group chat. ActivityRoster puts it all in one place.

See every RYA course set up with its staffing and ratios, instructors marked with the instructor types they hold, and Auto-roster fill a half-term week in seconds with people who are free and qualified. Clashes, missing licences and ratios are checked as you go, your team are told when each day is confirmed, and pay is worked out from the roster.
